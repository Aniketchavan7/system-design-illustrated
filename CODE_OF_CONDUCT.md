# Community Code of Conduct & Values

## Our Pledge

We are committed to making **System Design Illustrated** a friendly, helpful, and welcoming space for everyone, regardless of experience level, background, or identity.

Whether you are a student writing your very first lines of code, an engineer preparing for your next interview, or a seasoned systems architect, your voice and contributions are valued here.

## Our Community Standards

We encourage behaviors that foster an open and constructive learning environment:

- **Welcome mistakes with grace**: Distributed systems are hard and counter-intuitive. Nobody knows everything. When someone makes a typo, a factual error, or a flawed architectural assumption, treat it as a shared learning opportunity rather than a point of criticism.
- **Explain with empathy**: Favor simple language, concrete analogies, and patient explanations over elitism or jargon.
- **Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully**: Keep discussions focused on engineering tradeoffs, latency math, and hardware realities rather than personalities.
- **Show respect and curiosity**: Different companies face different constraints. What works at Google might fail at a resource-strapped startup, and vice versa. Value diverse engineering perspectives.

## Unacceptable Behavior

To protect our learning space, the following behaviors are not tolerated:

- Insulting, derogatory, or demeaning comments.
- Harassment or discrimination of any form.
- Trolling or disruptive arguments that do not contribute to constructive technical discussion.
- Plagiarism or publishing private information without explicit permission.

## Reporting & Moderation

If you encounter behavior that violates these principles, please reach out to the repository maintainers through GitHub Issues or via email to the maintainer. We will review concerns promptly, respectfully, and fairly.

Let us build, learn, and grow together as an open engineering community.
