Strong read-after-write overlap requires W + R > N. High availability under node crashes requires healthy replicas N − F ≥ W and N − F ≥ R.

Predict the trade-off

With N = 5, W = 3, and R = 3, what happens to reads and writes if a datacenter outage knocks 3 replicas offline?

Reveal the worked example

Only 5 − 3 = 2 replicas remain reachable. Since 2 < 3, both W = 3 writes and R = 3 reads are blocked (choosing Consistency over Availability). Lowering to W = 1, R = 1 preserves availability at the cost of stale reads.
